RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 1st January 2024, between:
LANDLORD: Mr. Ramesh Kumar, residing at 12 MG Road, Bengaluru - 560001
TENANT: Ms. Priya Sharma, residing at 45 Koramangala, Bengaluru - 560034

1. PREMISES
The Landlord agrees to rent the property located at Flat 3B, Green Valley Apartments, Koramangala 5th Block, Bengaluru - 560095 ("Premises") to the Tenant.

2. TERM
This Agreement shall commence on 1st February 2024 and continue for a period of eleven (11) months, unless terminated earlier as provided herein.

3. RENT
The monthly rent shall be Rs. 25,000 (Rupees Twenty-Five Thousand only), payable on or before the 5th day of each month. A late payment fee of Rs. 500 per day shall be charged for any payment received after the 5th of the month.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 1,00,000 (Rupees One Lakh only) at the time of signing this Agreement. This deposit shall be refunded within 30 days of vacating the Premises, subject to deduction for damages.

5. LOCK-IN PERIOD
The Tenant agrees to occupy the Premises for a minimum lock-in period of six (6) months from the commencement date. If the Tenant vacates before the lock-in period, the security deposit shall be forfeited.

6. NOTICE PERIOD
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving two (2) months written notice to the other party.

7. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for day-to-day maintenance and minor repairs up to Rs. 2,000. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs and major maintenance.

8. SUBLETTING
The Tenant shall not sublet or assign the Premises or any part thereof without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Violation of this clause shall result in immediate termination of this Agreement.

9. UTILITIES
The Tenant shall be responsible for all utility charges including electricity, water, and internet. Society maintenance charges of Rs. 3,000 per month shall also be paid by the Tenant.

10. LANDLORD'S RIGHT TO ENTER
The Landlord or his authorised representative may enter the Premises for inspection purposes with 24 hours prior written notice, except in cases of emergency.

11. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this Agreement immediately, without notice, if the Tenant:
(a) Fails to pay rent for more than 15 days;
(b) Uses the Premises for any unlawful purpose;
(c) Causes substantial damage to the Premises.

12. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes shall be subject to the jurisdiction of courts in Bengaluru.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first written above.
