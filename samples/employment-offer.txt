We are pleased to offer you the position of Software Engineer (L3) at TechCorp India Pvt. Ltd. ("Company"), subject to the terms and conditions set out in this letter.

1. POSITION AND DEPARTMENT
You will be joining as a Software Engineer in the Product Engineering team, reporting to the Engineering Manager. Your primary work location will be the Company's office in Pune, Maharashtra.

2. COMPENSATION
Your annual Cost to Company (CTC) will be Rs. 12,00,000 (Rupees Twelve Lakhs), structured as follows:
- Fixed Base Salary: Rs. 8,40,000 per annum
- Variable Performance Bonus: Up to Rs. 1,20,000 per annum (subject to performance)
- Employer PF Contribution: Rs. 1,08,000 per annum
- Other benefits: Rs. 32,000 per annum

3. PROBATION PERIOD
You will be on probation for a period of six (6) months from your date of joining. During probation, either party may terminate this arrangement by giving two (2) weeks notice. The Company reserves the right to extend the probation period at its sole discretion.

4. NOTICE PERIOD
After successful completion of probation, either party may terminate this employment by providing ninety (90) days written notice, or payment of salary in lieu thereof. The Company may waive the notice period at its discretion.

5. SERVICE BOND
You agree to serve the Company for a minimum period of two (2) years from your date of joining. If you resign or are terminated for cause before completing this period, you shall pay the Company Rs. 2,00,000 (Rupees Two Lakhs) as liquidated damages for training and recruitment costs.

6. NON-COMPETE
For a period of one (1) year after the cessation of your employment, you shall not directly or indirectly engage in, or be employed by, any business that is in direct competition with the Company's core business in India.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All inventions, works, discoveries, or developments made by you during your employment, whether or not during working hours, shall be the exclusive property of the Company.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY
You shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding all Company information, trade secrets, and client data during and after your employment with no time limitation.

9. BACKGROUND VERIFICATION
This offer is conditional upon satisfactory completion of a background verification check. The Company reserves the right to withdraw this offer if the verification reveals any discrepancy.

10. WORKING HOURS
Your standard working hours will be 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. You may be required to work additional hours as per business requirements without additional compensation.

This offer is valid for 7 days from the date of this letter. Please sign and return one copy.
